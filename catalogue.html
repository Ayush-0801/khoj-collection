Sambalpuri Bandha Saree

Bandha silk · Bargarh

In bandha the thread is tied and dyed before a single pick is woven, on both warp and weft. The weaver is matching two dyed patterns to each other as the cloth grows, which is why the edges of the motif are soft rather than sharp.
